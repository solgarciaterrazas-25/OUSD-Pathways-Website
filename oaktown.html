Start Your Journey in Education

OakTown Leaders is Oakland Unified School District's pre-apprenticeship program for current high school students who are interested in careers in education and community leadership.

Through dual enrollment college courses, paid internships, and mentorship, students gain valuable experience while building a strong foundation for future careers serving Oakland's children and families.

Graduates leave the program with college credit, professional experience, and a direct pathway into educator careers with OUSD.

Program Benefits

🎓 College Courses

Complete dual enrollment coursework while attending high school.

💵 Paid Stipends

Receive increasing stipends for each successfully completed college course.

☀️ Paid Internships

Gain meaningful work experience through paid summer internships.

🤝 Student Support

Academic advising, tutoring, books, laptops, and individualized case management throughout the program.

COLLEGE COURSEWORK

Earn College Credit While in High School

Students complete one dual enrollment college course each semester while still enrolled in high school, earning Early Childhood Education units that count toward future educator permits.

  • ✔ One dual enrollment college course every semester
  • ✔ Earn up to 12 Early Childhood Education units
  • ✔ College credits transferable toward educator pathways

PAID INTERNSHIPS

Gain Real Classroom Experience

Apprentices participate in two paid summer internships, working alongside educators while developing leadership, communication, and professional skills.

  • ✔ Two paid summer internships
  • ✔ $1,000 stipend each summer
  • ✔ Hands-on experience working with children
  • ✔ Mentorship from experienced educators

Who Can Apply?

Current Oakland Unified School District high school students interested in education, leadership, and serving their community.

What You'll Earn

  • Instructional Assistant Permit (6 ECE Units)
  • Associate Teacher Permit Pathway (12 ECE Units)
  • Paid Internship Experience
  • College Credits

After Graduation

  • Paraeducator
  • Early Literacy Tutor
  • ECE Instructional Assistant
  • Eligible for the Early Educator Apprenticeship
